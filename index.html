Lighthaven Books

Drag books onto shelves or bookcases. Click to edit, double-click a shelved book to flip spine/cover.

Import books

Paste CSV/TSV (e.g. copied from Google Sheets) or pick a file. Header row required. Recognized columns: title, author, and optionally thickness/spine (in), width/cover width (in), height (in), orientation (spine/cover), room, bookcase, shelf (number), notes. Other columns are kept as notes.
